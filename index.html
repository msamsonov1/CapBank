Planetary Subsurface Percussive Discharge & Resonant Inversion Physics

Deep subsurface planetary drills (e.g., Artemis ice exploration, Martian crustal sampling) utilize high-voltage capacitor discharge modules discharging tens of kiloamperes into giant-magnetostrictive or piezoelectric rock-fracturing stacks. Under sustained 10,000g percussive shock waves, capacitor banks suffer progressive internal foil contact degradation. The CapBank software executes a real-time cycle-by-cycle system identification model that audits bank integrity through transient ring-down dynamics.

1. Damped Underdamped Ringing Dynamics

The primary discharge circuit is modeled as a series RLC transient loop where the capacitor energy discharges across rock-fracturing piezoelectric transducer load:

$$L_{\text{loop}} \frac{d^2 i(t)}{dt^2} + R_{\text{esr}} \frac{di(t)}{dt} + \frac{1}{C_{\text{eff}}} i(t) = 0$$

The ringing natural angular frequency $\omega_d$ and damping attenuation coefficient $\alpha$ govern the measured oscilloscope waveform:

$$\omega_0 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{L_{\text{loop}} C_{\text{eff}}}}, \quad \alpha = \frac{R_{\text{esr}}}{2 L_{\text{loop}}}, \quad \omega_d = \sqrt{\omega_0^2 - \alpha^2}$$

The peak discharge current attained on initial rock fracturing strike satisfies:

$$I_{\text{peak}} \approx V_0 \sqrt{\frac{C_{\text{eff}}}{L_{\text{loop}}}} \exp\left(-\frac{\alpha}{\omega_d}\arctan\left(\frac{\omega_d}{\alpha}\right)\right)$$
Audit Objective: Compute $L_{\text{loop}}$ and $C_{\text{eff}}$ independently from a single current ring-down pulse.
2. Foil Decoupling & Lead Micro-Crack Geometry

Capacitor units consist of segmented metallized polypropylene or ceramic dielectrics. Extreme shock waves cause:

  • Sector Disconnection: High mechanical vibration shatters schoopage end-spray bonds, isolating parallel sectors:
    $$C_{\text{eff}} = \sum_{k=1}^{N_{\text{intact}}} C_k = N_{\text{intact}} \cdot C_{\text{sector}}$$
  • Inductance Loop Elongation: Lead fatigue and localized conductor necking force the high-frequency pulsed current around torn foil perimeters, altering parasitic magnetic path integral:
    $$L_{\text{loop}} = \mu_0 \oint_{S} \frac{\mathbf{A} \cdot d\boldsymbol{\ell}}{I} \approx L_0 + \Delta L_{\text{path}} \left(\frac{N_{\text{failed}}}{N_{\text{total}}}\right)$$
Observation: As $C$ drops, $L$ counter-intuitively rises due to elongated return paths.
3. Online System Identification Algorithm

On every percussive strike, high-speed Rogowski coils sample zero-crossings ($t_1, t_2$) and consecutive positive peak amplitudes ($I_1, I_2$):

$$\delta = \ln\left(\frac{I_1}{I_2}\right) = \alpha T_d = \frac{\pi R_{\text{esr}}}{\omega_d L_{\text{loop}}}, \quad T_d = 2(t_2 - t_1)$$

The observer software solves the closed-form dual parameter inversion:

$$L_{\text{loop}} = \frac{V_0}{I_1 \omega_d} \exp\left(-\frac{\delta}{2\pi} \arctan\left(\frac{2\pi}{\delta}\right)\right)$$
$$C_{\text{eff}} = \frac{1}{L_{\text{loop}} \left(\omega_d^2 + \left(\frac{\delta}{T_d}\right)^2\right)}$$

4. Closed-Loop PRF Derating Failsafe

When sectors disconnect, the remaining capacitors absorb higher $dV/dt$ stresses. If pulse repetition frequency (PRF) is maintained, thermal avalanche causes total bank explosion:

$$P_{\text{loss}} = f_{\text{PRF}} \cdot E_{\text{pulse}} = f_{\text{PRF}} \left(\frac{1}{2} C_{\text{eff}} V_0^2\right)$$

To keep thermal dissipation below the critical dielectric breakdown limit $P_{\text{crit}}$, the CapBank controller automatically modulates driver frequency:

$$f_{\text{derated}} = f_{\text{nominal}} \cdot \left(\frac{C_{\text{eff}}}{C_{\text{nominal}}}\right)^2 \cdot \left(\frac{L_{\text{nominal}}}{L_{\text{loop}}}\right)$$

This preserves penetration capability at reduced speed while preventing permanent hardware destruction.
